Best Movies according to Prerna

My top 3 Movies

3 Idiots

Three Idiots is a highly acclaimed movie that combines humor, emotion, and a powerful message about education and following one’s passion. It highlights the pressure students face in a competitive system and encourages thinking creatively rather than memorizing blindly. With strong performances, heartfelt moments, and a meaningful story, it remains one of the best and most inspiring films in Indian cinema.

Chak De India

Chak De! India is an inspiring sports drama that tells the story of a former hockey player, Kabir Khan, who redeems himself by coaching the Indian women's hockey team to victory. The film promotes themes of teamwork, national pride, and breaking stereotypes. With powerful performances and a gripping storyline, it motivates viewers to believe in second chances and the strength of unity.

Taare Zameen Par

Taare Zameen Par is a heartwarming film that sheds light on the struggles of a dyslexic child, Ishaan, who faces difficulty in a traditional education system. Directed by Aamir Khan, the movie beautifully explores the importance of understanding, empathy, and recognizing individual talents. It’s a poignant reminder that every child learns differently, and with the right support, they can truly shine. The film resonates deeply with anyone who’s ever felt misunderstood.
